## Training — 3 evening sessions

Hotel gym is almost certainly basic (third-party sources: "compact," cardio + a few free
weights). **Assume no Smith machine, no cable stack, no leg curl/extension, no hack
squat.** Will decided to work with whatever's there — no day pass, no bands.

All sessions **evening**. A heavy 6am session before a 9-hour day he's *leading* trades a
marginal stimulus for real sharpness risk, and Schofield's framework doesn't require AM
training.

| Day | Split | Work |
|---|---|---|
| **Tue eve** (best slot) | Torso | DB Bench 4×8–15 @8–9 · One-Arm DB Row 3–4×10–15 @9 · DB Pullover 3×15–20 · DB Shoulder Press 3×10–15 |
| **Wed eve** | Legs | DB Goblet/Front-Rack Squat 4×12–20 · DB RDL 3×10–15 @9 · Bulgarian Split Squat 3×10–15/leg · Walking Lunge 3×12–16/leg · Calf Raise 3×15–25 |
| **Thu eve** | Arms | Hammer Curl 3×12–20 · Incline Curl 3×12–15 · OH Triceps Ext 3×12–20 · Lateral Raise 3×15–20 · Upright Row 3×12–15 — isolations to failure |

**Why higher reps are correct, not a compromise** ([[schofield]]): 5–30 reps roughly equal
for hypertrophy; "dumbbells more gym-friendly than barbells when crowded… lower volume is
fine, push harder per set." A 40lb DB squat at 12–20 near failure is a legitimate stimulus.
Auto-regulate set ranges — trend low if fatigued.

**Two don'ts:**
1. Don't chase 145lb Larsen / Smith Squat numbers with dumbbells — different exercise, not
   a load comparison.
2. **Don't treat this week as canary data.** Thresholds are calibrated to specific
   barbell/machine loads; resume canary testing at home.

No session Friday (6:35am departure).
