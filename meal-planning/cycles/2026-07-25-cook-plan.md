**4. Chicken parm (marinara-baked)** *(breast pack #2 — LAST; reuse the sandwich breading station)*
- Breast pack #2, breaded in the same station, baked in **[BUY] marinara** + **[BUY] mozzarella** + **[BUY] Parmigiano**, over **ziti/pasta** (pantry).
- ~4 servings: shared Mon dinner ×2 + Elena Mon M2 + Elena Tue M2.

**5. Three side preps (SAT — each pairs to a meal cooked today):**
- **Cucumber kimchi** → pairs with the **sandwiches**. English cucumber sliced, salt 20 min, drain; toss with gochugaru + fish sauce + garlic + rice vinegar + sesame. (Full recipe given earlier.)
- **Roasted zucchini** → pairs with the **rotisserie plate**. Sliced, tossed in oil + S&P + garlic powder, 425°F ~20 min till caramelized. (Can share oven time with the egg bake / parm.)
- **Kale — Tuscan garlic-parm sauté** (the "make it taste great" version, uses on-hand Parmigiano):
  1. Strip kale off stems, tear, wash (leave water clinging).
  2. Hot pan, 1 Tbsp olive oil, **3 cloves garlic** sliced + a pinch **red pepper flake**, 20 sec.
  3. Kale in, toss 4–5 min till wilted; splash of water if it catches.
  4. Off heat: squeeze of **lemon**, grate **Parmigiano** over, S&P. (Optional crunch: toasted pine nuts or leftover cornflake crumbs.) The lemon + parm is what makes it restaurant-good, not diet kale.

**6. Dry-brine the steaks (SAT NIGHT — for Sunday's sear)**
- Salt both steaks generously all over, set on a rack/plate **uncovered in the fridge overnight**. Dries the surface for a better crust + seasons through. Sear them Sunday (step 7 below).
