# Cycle 2026-08-05 — Cook Plan (Wed 8/5 → Tue 8/11, ad hoc post-NYC / pre-business-trip)

Spread across the week rather than one big weekend session — Will's call, since rotisserie
moved to Wednesday and closed the Thu/Fri M3 gap that would otherwise have been open.

## Wednesday 8/5 (tonight) — Curry + Rotisserie

1. **Curry first** (longest, most steps): soak red lentils 15 min → make the paste
   (tomatoes, ginger powder, garlic, chili powder, coconut-milk-cream skimmed from the can,
   salt, pepper) → temper mustard seeds + ground cumin + turmeric in olive oil → cook paste
   3-4 min → add lentils + water + coconut milk + chicken stock, simmer 15 min → add 22oz
   chicken breast + green beans, simmer 10-12 min → wilt in spinach → serve over rice.
   22oz chicken breast (1 Costco pack) — the other pack is mac & cheese's, not this recipe's.
2. **Rotisserie in parallel** (mostly assembly, run alongside the curry's simmer time):
   portion the Costco rotisserie chicken into 5, cook rice (0.3 cup/serving), whisk the
   sriracha-yogurt sauce (Greek yogurt + sriracha + lime), zucchini side. No garlic in this
   one anymore.
3. **Shake bags** (~10 min, do during downtime): portion whey + frozen mixed fruit + frozen
   spinach + frozen banana + flax/chia blend into 7 freezer bags. Add milk + Greek yogurt +
   creatine + PB2 fresh each morning, don't pre-blend.

**Result:** M3 covered starting tomorrow (rotisserie), M1 covered all week (shake bags),
partial M2/M4 coverage from curry.

## Thursday 8/6 — Burritos + prep-ahead for Friday

1. **Burritos in the slow cooker FIRST** — 22oz chicken thighs + taco seasoning + lime
   juice + chicken bouillon + green enchilada sauce + garlic/salt. HIGH 2-3hr or LOW 3-4hr.
   Mostly unattended — start it, let it run through the evening.
2. **While that's running — prep Friday's teriyaki:** marinate the 6 thighs (dark soy +
   light soy + garlic + Splenda + five spice + white pepper). Overnight beats the recipe's
   30-min minimum — better flavor by tomorrow.
3. **Also while it's running — prep Friday's kimchi:** salt-drain the Persian cucumbers
   15-20 min, toss with gochugaru + fish sauce + garlic + rice vinegar + sesame seeds.
   Improves overnight, so Thursday-night prep beats same-day. This one batch covers BOTH
   teriyaki (Friday) and fried rice (Saturday) — don't make it twice.
4. **Once the slow cooker's done:** shred the chicken + mix filling (rice, diced veg,
   cilantro, lime, Greek yogurt, cheddar, mozzarella) → chill until thick → weigh, divide by
   11 → roll into burritos with the low-carb tortillas → freeze. Only 5 of the 11 are this
   cycle's (2 M3 + 3 M4); the other 6 are standing freezer stock for future weeks.

## Friday 8/7 — Teriyaki (already marinated) + day-old rice for Saturday

1. **Air fry the marinated thighs** — 360°F, 17 min. No bok choy/gai lan side anymore —
   serve sliced thighs + rice + the kimchi made last night instead.
2. **Cook the day-old rice for Saturday's fried rice** — just a rice-cooker run (1.8 cups
   dry), spread on a tray, chill uncovered in the fridge overnight. Has to happen tonight
   specifically — fried rice needs it chilled, not fresh.
3. **Pre-soak Saturday's sweet potato fries** — cut into fries (max 1/2"x1/4"x3.5"), soak
   in cold water 15-30 min, drain, refrigerate overnight. Skips a step Saturday — go
   straight to dry → cornstarch → air fry tomorrow.

---

## Saturday 8/8 — Mac & Cheese + Fried Rice + Sweet Potato Fries (lighter day)

1. **Fried rice**: scramble egg whites + whole eggs in the deep dish cast iron skillet,
   set aside. Sear diced thighs, set aside. Stir-fry garlic + ginger + green onion whites +
   frozen edamame (straight in, no thaw). Add the cold day-old rice, crisp 3-4 min. Add
   soy + oyster sauce + sesame oil + white pepper. Return egg + chicken + green onion
   greens. Garnish cilantro. Serve with the kimchi from Thursday night on the side.
2. **Mac & cheese**: cook 4 Goodles boxes per package (boil water, cook pasta, stir in
   cheese packet) — do in batches if one pot's too small. Sear the 22oz chicken breast
   (diced, salt/pepper/garlic powder). Combine pasta + chicken. Veg (peas/carrots/mixed) is
   optional, added per-bowl at serving, not mixed into the batch.
3. **Sweet potato fries**: dry the pre-soaked fries completely, toss oil → cornstarch →
   salt/seasoning, air fry 400°F ~12 min in batches (don't crowd), +2 min for extra crisp.
4. **While the above are going — batch-prep for next time:** portion and freeze the
   surplus chicken thighs from the Costco case (only ~3 of the 6 sub-packs get used this
   week between teriyaki/fried rice/burritos — split and label the rest rather than leaving
   one undivided case in the freezer).
