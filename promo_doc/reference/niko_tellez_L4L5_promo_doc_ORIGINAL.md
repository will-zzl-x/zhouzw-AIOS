Scope of role
As a Supply Chain Manager II (SCMII) within IPEX-Transfers Orchestration (TORCH), the *New Transfer-In (NTI) Network Health/ Steering Orchestration* role is an individual contributor, where the processes and functions to achieve the strategy are not well defined. Exceptional understanding of the NTI Supply Chain is required to guide tactical stakeholder group(s) via leading multiple cross-functional teams as the primary escalation point up to three (3) levels above their own, analyzing existing processes and implementing improvements based on data analysis, and knowing how/ when to appropriately push back with data to curb egregious spend on Redirect Execution ($12.6M/ Year). For example, Virginia Regional IXD ORF2 consistently missed their NTI Receipts (-7M Units /-73% Light), and carton plans, from WK25-26 2025, pushing for an 800K Redirect solve in WK27. Rather than blanket approve the request, the SCMII can flag the incongruency in receipts VS density, escalate to the appropriate stakeholder group(s) and push for a more proactive solution. *The SCMII is responsible for the NTI Network Health/Steering for 120 sites, equating to 342M executed units per week or 36% of the Inbound (IB) Supply Chain*. Given the broad nature of this space, the SCMII effectively partners with Analytics to develop Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) that meaningfully surface the _right_ day-over-day call outs to help guide tactical decision making. Exceptional executional trends are documented and skillfully escalated for strategic support, where the SCMII is starting to contribute to strategic initiatives via limited document writing and forward-looking metric development. For example, Texas AR-Sortable (AR-S) building SAT3 has experienced two (2) separate instances of multi-week bullwhip requests in 2025, where the SCMII owns a mechanism to flag these bullwhips and proactively share with the strategic arm of IPEX-TORCH. *This role brings clarity to a largely ambiguous space via constantly auditing existing ownership guardrails and effectively delegating the right actions (both internally and externally) to optimize for the organizational goal of reducing AR-S NTI Redirect Spend YoY -$4M / -95%*. Additionally, the SCMII is responsible for the NTI Steering Tenets/Guardrails and holding internal/external partners to the same high bar of adhering to these tenets, however, they can autonomously identify when a proposed plan appears risky and arrange for additional reviews accordingly. Finally, the SCMII is extremely effective at verbal and written communication, where this skillset is regularly demonstrated in large setting Daily/Weekly Business Review(s) and supporting critical operational escalations with high *Quality*, *Speed* and *Tact*.

Evidence
AR-S Standard Work for Redirects (Artifact #1)
In 2024 IPEX approved/ executed 135M Redirect Units in the AR-S Network, incurring a total cost of $4.2M. Year to date, 12M Units in AR-S Redirects have been approved/ executed, where the team is on track to the year end glidepath of 34M Units, a nearly 4X reduction YoY and equating to $4M in cost avoidance. The key operational difference between the 2024 and 2025 calendar years was driven by the design and implementation of an AR-S Steering Standard Work which was developed and published by Nik (*Deliver Results*). The document outlines the comprehensive processes for managing Sortable, TSSL, and SDC Network type(s) focusing on redirect requests, escalations, and network optimization. Specifically, Nik conducted extensive stakeholder syncs to establish data-driven thresholds, analyzed historical redirect patterns, and collaborated with cross-functional teams to *develop and implement the standard work with internal/ external partners.* As a result of the new standard cross-functional alignment/ approval, Nik was able to implement stricter requirements for Giver/ Taker sites to exhaust proactive labor levers (e.g. VET, MET, labor sharing) prior to requesting a redirect, maximizing MMV execution (70-95%) and optimizing site fullness (85-95%), whereas the previous standards lacked data driven consistent thresholds for review/ approval leading to egregious redirect execution (*Insist on the Highest Standards*). As a result, the number of ad-hoc SIMs requested by S&amp;OP has reduced -72% YoY as external entities are calibrating their requests to the Standard Work guidelines prior to submitting a SIM. Outside of methodically holding external teams to a high bar for requests, Nik shows a high degree of professionalism and tact when pushing for proactive levers in public forums with many differing views, such as, Daily/ Monthly Business Reviews and Email Communication (*Earn Trust*). *Nik can be trusted to deliver the messaging required to meet the strategic vision of our organization with speed, care and deep knowledge of the selected situation*.
*Leadership Principle(s) demonstrated:* Earn Trust, Insist on the Highest Standards, Deliver Results
Leveraging TOMY Transportation for AR-S Redirects (Artifact #2)
Outside of the potential for lead-time degradation, the second most unfavorable attribute of a Redirect is the direct transportation costs incurred because of the movement. When Nik first started in the role, he would receive one-off chimes/ escalations from various leaders in S&amp;OP and NACF to leverage internal Amazon FC driver capacity (“TOMY”) where the direct transportation costs are considered sunk if not utilized on-shift. The process lacked end-to-end visibility, was disorganized, and often led to bullwhip movements of the same load. While technically Nik’s only responsibility was to action the TOMY drivers via VRID creation and follow the VRID through Taker delivery, he quickly identified several process gaps that needed to be addressed to enhance operational visibility (thus reducing time on task) and eliminate bullwhip movements (*Invent and Simplify*). Specifically, *Nik liaised between cross-functional teams (NACF, TOMY, S&amp;OP)* via establishing a weekly call and notional flow map, documenting limitations/ constraints with the existing process and proposing an ideal state where he secured buy-in from each responsible party. Since rolling out the SIM process, TOMY run success (defined as trailers departing/ arriving FCs same day) has improved from &lt;50% of movements to &gt;80%, avoiding $37K in redirect costs (*Frugality*). Moreover, TOMY has been utilized on 10.2% of all AR-S Redirects year to date (VS &lt;1% in 2025), further demonstrating the fidelity/ usability of the new fixed process.
*Leadership Principle(s) demonstrated:* Invent and Simplify, Frugality
Internal/ External Network Steering RACI (Artifact #3)
This year (2025) marked the first year Amazon completely transitioned to a Two-Tier Regionalized Inbound Network, which brought the conversion of 47 buildings into National or Regional IXD designation. With this transition, IPEX VSCOR/ TORCH teams were tasked with re-structuring to best align Program Management/ Strategic support to this vision. Nik was eager to support NTI within Regional IXD, however, ownership lines across internal/ external teams were often blurred leading to task redundancies and inadequate network health planning. As a result, Nik led the design and documentation of a Responsible, Accountable, Consulted, Informed (RACI) for Regional IXD, which comprises thirty-four (34) and forty-nine (49) unique tasks for NTI and NVF, respectively (*Ownership*). Additionally, the RACI captures the responsibilities of seven (7) internal/ external organizations. *It is estimated that because of the creation of this RACI, approximately thirteen (13) hours per week per stakeholder is saved driven by a reduction in redundant meeting preparation tasks and ad-hoc bridge requests*. Nik assembled this RACI via forming a deep understanding of tools utilized within the Regional IXD supply chain (outside of his direct responsibility), as well as, influencing/ guiding the development of this resource up to three (3) levels above his own.
*Leadership Principle(s) demonstrated:* Ownership
Regional IXD By-Pass Fixed Process (Artifact #4)
In line with the newness of Two-Tier Regionalization, and Nik’s established reputation as _the_ NTI STL for Regional IXD, Nik led the design and implementation of a fixed process for by-passing NTI from Regional IXD. Based on the Network Need input (e.g. High Regional IXD Backlog / Low AR-Sortable Backlog), Nik developed a comprehensive metric that captured National IXD departure/ arrival WAPE(s), WK+1 forward-looking receipt plans, DICE Limits (pre-DICE being turned ‘off’ for Regional IXD) and yard health/ fullness at the downstream Regional IXD (*Dive Deep*). Nik was able to develop this metric via asking the right questions to various owners/ influencers within the Regional IXD supply chain and effectively identifying risks for communication. Nik was asked to present this comprehensive metric in Daily/ Weekly Business Reviews as it was the sole file used to govern Regional IXD by-pass approvals up to the L8 level. *In total, 2.6k trailers / 56.9M units were successfully by-passed away from the Regional IXD Network at an incremental cost of $2.79M / $0.05c per unit, as a direct result of the process and metric(s) that Nik established*. This bypass initiative significantly reduced Regional IXD backlog by enabling direct shipment to AR-Sortable facilities, improving processing efficiency and reducing overall network congestion. Finally, Nik observed a pattern with select lanes where the lead time to bid acceptance was significantly higher than standard (&gt;48 hours from VRID creation). As a result, Nik partnered with Carrier Management to escalate rates on high impact lanes characterized by low bid acceptance rates, high volume throughput and extended length of haul, resulting in &lt;24 hour lead time to pickup from VRID creation.
