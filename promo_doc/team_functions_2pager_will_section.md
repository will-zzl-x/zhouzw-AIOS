## Full version

**Will Zhou — Redirect Cost Governance & Execution**

*Function owner for: warranting, costing, and executing IXD redirects at controlled spend
— plus the tooling and team capability that makes it repeatable.*

**1. Redirect budget guardrails (primary function).** Own the warrant standard for whether
a redirect is justified at all — the σ/W+1-backlog gate that says a lane only moves if the
giver is genuinely constrained and the backlog won't self-solve. Wired into the daily audit
and the weekly cap. This is the mechanism that keeps redirect spend defensible and is the
commitment carried up to Hartland/Prime. *(Load reduction is an output of this, not the goal.)*

**2. Redirect cost workflow.** Own per-lane costing — every SIM priced $/load against a
$3K/load escalation ceiling, with the zero-cost levers (AZIM midstream, ESS, TOMY) modeled
correctly so spend is neither overstated nor hidden. Gives leadership the true ICT cost of
the redirect book at any point (~$290K open today, none over ceiling).

**3. Continue/stop SIM governance.** Own the SIM auditor — evolving from "did it execute"
into "should we keep pushing this," flagging lanes to stop when the giver's no longer
constrained or the taker is filling. Turns execution tracking into a decision tool.

**4. Midstream reroute (AZIM) workstream + SOP.** Own the cheapest lever end-to-end —
standardizing reroutability classification (crosstown / rail-structure logic) into a
repeatable SOP so it scales beyond hand-vetting.

**5. Daily control-tower reporting.** Own the morning/afternoon flash — MDP, buffer, redirect
execution, and cost visibility the desk and leadership run on.

**6. External-partner programs.** Own the HJBI drop-to-live pilot ($22.5M opportunity,
J.B. Hunt) — designed and executed, concluding to lessons-learned + scalability.

**7. Team capability.** Ramping Jeff on the daily redirect review (Kiro SOP, tracking to
OLR); standing up the GenAI bootcamp for the L3–L4 cohort. Scales the decision-making off
my desk.

*(Optional #8 — DOE Redirect Automation: left out above because it may sit ABOVE team-function
level as a cross-org build. Add as a function if the 2-pager is meant to capture cross-org
initiatives too.)*
