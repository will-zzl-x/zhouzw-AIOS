# Ash

**Role in the story:** Rescued halfling child; emotional anchor for Laila's arc
**First appears:** The lab-dome rescue chapter (halfling children facility)

---

## The one-line version

A very young halfling child with a fire-based gift he can't yet control, rescued by Laila from the half-brother's facility — the only one of the rescued children not stolen from the Rogue, found on the street instead, and named "Ash" by the other child prisoners during captivity.

## Surface

- Toddler-aged ("the young one," per the drafted pages). Fire-based gift — described as "fire in his veins," eyes that glow red with hints of gold in the dark, heat that seeps into his fingers when his gift stirs.
- Not yet able to control it — Laila hushes him to "keep it contained" while carrying him through the facility, echoing (consciously or not) the exact kind of self-restraint she's about to watch James's own containment mirror later.
- Named "Ash" by the other imprisoned children, not by any adult — he had no name before that, or none that's known.
