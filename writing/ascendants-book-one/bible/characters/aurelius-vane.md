## The one-line version

The pure-blood dictator whose gift is absolute mental subjugation; he's built an entire regime and classification system to collect every pure-blood on the planet, and Laila is the one person his system — and his control — can't touch.

- Runs the Guard as a militia-style dictatorship: pure-bloods and humans only in his army; he considers half-breeds not worth his time or attention.
- How a stranger would describe him: clinical, imperial, obsessed with total order.
