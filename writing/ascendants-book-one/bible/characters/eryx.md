# Eryx (goes by "Eris" to Laila)

**Role in the story:** Laila's mentor/jailer-figure; morally grey ally
**First appears:** Chapter 1, as "Eris"

---

## The one-line version

A half-breed Rogue infiltrator with a cloning gift, who spent years undercover as "Eris" — having infiltrated the Guard — training Laila under a false identity, until a hired raid he orchestrated to extract her went genuinely wrong.

## Surface

- A single sliver of brilliant gold in one eye — his true half-breed mark. His "Eris" clone-identity wears a different eye color to hide it.
- Large, physically imposing; "large arms," "thick eyebrows" (manuscript, as "Eryx" at the docks).
- How a stranger would describe him after five minutes: brilliant, pragmatic, morally grey, prone to needling humor to defuse tension — but capable of real coldness when focused on an objective.

## Contradiction

Genuinely loves/cares for Laila (spent years training and protecting her as "Eris") while also being willing to use her as a pawn without her consent, believing the ends justify it. The gentleness Laila knew as "Eris" may be a facet of him that's now permanently gone, since that specific clone died and can't be brought back without blowing his cover.

## The cloning gift, specifically

- Each clone is genuinely *him* — piloted directly, not a puppet. What a clone says or does, he is doing in that moment.
- He can vary surface details between clones and his true self (e.g., "Eris"'s eye color conceals his real half-breed mark).
- He infiltrated the Guard as "Eris" specifically — meaning Laila's own household security was compromised from the inside for years.
- The "Eris" clone died for real during the villa raid because the Valsk crew he hired to fake the kidnapping turned on him — this wasn't the planned, controlled sacrifice; it was a genuine loss forced by a betrayal outside his control.
- Has never cloned anyone but himself before Book 1's climax, when he clones Laila (to confuse a tracker sent by Vane) — his first-ever attempt at cloning someone else.
