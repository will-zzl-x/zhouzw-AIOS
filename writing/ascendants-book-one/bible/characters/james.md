# James

**Role in the story:** Deuteragonist & love interest
**First appears:** Chapter 2

---

## The one-line version

A pure-blood carrying the rare, reassembled core essence of the Protector — a gift (Extraction/Consumption) that has driven every previous host in history mad and killed them, which he's spent his life containing as "the Beast."

## Surface

- Emerald green eyes, unique among all living Ascendants — they burn intensely when his gift activates. The classification system has no category for his eye color, so he's historically been dismissed as fully human, letting him hide in plain sight.
- Strong jaw, permanently concentrated/reproachful brow, short dark hair. Trained fighter — disarms multiple opponents with ease, fights left-handed when his right shoulder is injured.
- How a stranger would describe him after five minutes: quietly dangerous, dryly funny with people he trusts (Katiana, Eryx), guarded and physically tense around unexpected touch/closeness.

## Interior

- **What he wants:** *(open)*
- **What he actually needs:** *(open)*
- **What he's afraid of:** losing control of the Beast and becoming what every previous host became — a mad, world-ending thing that consumes every gift in reach.
- **The lie he believes:** *(open — likely some version of "I am the monster my father tried to make me," worth confirming rather than assuming)*
- **The wound:** bred by his own father (a Valsk clan leader) as an attempted weapon; dismissed as "giftless" as a child; his father experimented on him trying to force a gift out; when his father decided to kill him and his mother instead, the Beast manifested for the first time and killed much of the camp, including the father. He and his mother escaped to the Rogue — the only pure-blood the Rogue has ever taken in.

## Under pressure

- Frightened: locks the Beast behind a "steel door" through sheer willpower and discipline; visibly braces (hand on wall, arm across chest) when it's fighting to get out.
- Gets power: *(open — has never let the Beast out on purpose since the massacre, as far as established)*
- Would betray someone he loves: *(open)*
- Line he won't cross: doesn't kill when he doesn't have to — established at the end of Book 1 when he extracts a tracker's gift rather than killing him. Circumstance that could push him over it: *(open, but likely tied to Laila's safety)*

## Contradiction

Fiercely protective and gentle with the vulnerable (Laila, Ceryne, Katiana's past) — while housing, and constantly restraining, the single most destructive force in the world's history. Presents as "giftless"/ordinary by design, while being the rarest and most dangerous Ascendant alive.

## Arc

- **Where he starts (emotionally):** in exile from his own worst moment, believing containment of the Beast is the only thing keeping him human.
- **Where he ends:** *(open)*
- **The moment it turns:** likely tied to meeting Laila, whose presence makes the Beast react like never before. The reunion with his mother at the Rogue after the lab-dome rescue is a strong candidate for where Laila (and the reader) first sees him without his guard up — worth deciding if this is *the* turn or one of several.
