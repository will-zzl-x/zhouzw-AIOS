## The one-line version

Leader of the Rogue and one of the last two Logai — a near-extinct race that held total, inherited knowledge of the past — whose twin sister was captured by Aurelius Vane and now unwillingly built the very system that fails to classify Laila.

## Surface

- Mischievous emerald eyes (per James's memory), described as glistening "with power and of course, lust."
- Runs an organization that takes in girls and boys of all ages and gives them better lives than the streets of the underworld would.
- How a stranger would describe her: playful, seductive persona ("Am I not unlike the great Mother, my dear Jamie?") masking a leader who's survived a genocide and lost her twin.

## Contradiction

Plays the flirtatious "Madame Teresa" persona to the outside world while carrying the weight of a genocide, a captured twin, and leadership of the entire resistance.

## The Logai, specifically

- Held total, passive, inherited knowledge of the past, passed down generation to generation — near-extinct.
- Genocide occurred during Tessa's childhood, tied to Vane's rise to power; his goal was to kill them all.
- Tessa and her twin sister are the only known survivors.
- The twin was captured and kept by Vane (rather than killed) because Tessa herself was still free — presumably as leverage or for her knowledge.
- Tessa severed the sisters' shared-knowledge connection at some point; their knowledge has diverged since.
- The twin has proven extremely useful to Vane — she built the categorization system used to classify every pure-blood Ascendant. That system fails, for the first time ever, on Laila.
- Flagged in conversation as possible **Book 4 prequel** material.
