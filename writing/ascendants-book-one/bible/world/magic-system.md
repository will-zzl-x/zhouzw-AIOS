## The one-line version

Ascendants are humans born with fragments of a shattered primordial power ("gifts") — nearly all carry a gold-eyed shard of the shattered **Protector**; exactly one living person (Laila) carries the **Mother's** gift, which was never split.

## Origin myth: the Primordial Tragedy

Before Ascendants existed, two primordial entities maintained cosmic balance:

- **The Mother of Vision** — complete, undivided, uncorrupted Sight. Absolute awareness, selfless.
- **The Protector** — raw preservation and energy containment, with a fatal embedded flaw: Greed/Consumption.

When a catastrophic threat arose, the Protector's need to shield the world twisted into desperation — he tried to consume the Mother's Sight to gain total power. To stop him, the Mother shattered his soul into thousands of fragments, destroying both of their original forms.

- **Ascendants** are the scattered pieces of the Protector's shattered power, dispersed into humanity. Every pure-blood/half-breed gift and its gold-eye marker is a mutated fragment of his one original gift.
- **The Mother's gift was never split.** It stayed whole and dormant for centuries. Laila is the first and only person, ever, to carry it.
- **The Protector's core essence rarely reassembles** into a single host instead of staying scattered — manifesting as a dark phenomenon marked by green eyes and a drive to *take* gifts back. Every historical host before James was consumed by "the Beast": took too many gifts, went mad, and died.

## Eye-color spectrum (the classification key)

- **Full, solid gold eyes** = pure-blood.
- **Partial gold** (a sliver, or one gold eye and one not) = half-breed ("halfling").
- **No gold at all** = presumed fully human by the classification system — this is how James hides in plain sight; his eyes are emerald, a color the system has no category for.
- **Emerald green, burning when active** = James's specific hallmark, as the Protector's reassembled core essence.
- **Solid, unnaturally glowing gold** = Aurelius Vane's hallmark.
- **A single sliver of gold in one eye** = Eryx's true half-breed mark (his "Eris" clone wears a different eye color to conceal it).
- Laila's eyes fade fully to black when her gift drains her, returning with a razor-thin, brilliant gold ring of absolute clarity.

## Rules

- **Who can use it / how is it determined:** born randomly to human parents, no predictable bloodline — part of why Ascendants are historically feared as sudden monsters.
- **Established individual gifts so far:**
  - **Laila** — absolute predictive Sight (reads physical vectors, micro-expressions, muscle twitches, atmospheric cues to calculate probability). Manifested young and raw: as a toddler she could locate people inside their own homes, before any trained/refined form of the gift. Also, not yet known to her: she can *project* her gift onto others, letting them use their own gift at full capacity — effectively awakening a half-breed toward pure-blood-level power. Hinted throughout the trilogy; pays off as a major moment in Book 3's final war.
  - **James** — Extraction/Consumption: can take another Ascendant's gift outright, leaving them alive but powered-down ("basically useless"), not killed.
  - **Eryx** — Cloning: each clone is genuinely *him*, piloted directly (not a puppet) — what a clone says or does, he is doing. He can vary surface details (e.g. eye color) between his true self and a clone. Has never cloned anyone but himself before Book 1's climax, when he clones Laila.
  - **Aurelius Vane** — total mental subjugation/control. Does not work on Laila — his single greatest frustration.
  - **Tessa (Logai)** — not a "gift" in the Ascendant sense; total, passive, inherited knowledge of the past.

## Learning curve

- Ascendant gifts seem to manifest or intensify under extremity (James's Beast first surfaced to save his mother; Laila's projection gift is dormant even to herself).
- The Rogue trains half-breeds creatively to use partial gifts at their fullest despite lacking full pure-blood power — a strategic edge Vane has dismissively overlooked because he considers half-breeds beneath his notice.

## Society's relationship to it

- Historically, Ascendants are feared as monsters — cautionary folklore for human children. Most Ascendant children are abandoned in infancy by frightened human parents.
- In the present day, a system (incentive- or mandate-based — shape not yet decided) encourages humans to turn pure-blood Ascendants over to Aurelius Vane's regime.
- Vane's regime (the Guard / the army) is built from pure-bloods and humans only; he considers half-breeds not worth his time or army.
- The Rogue is the counter-model: mostly half-breeds, run as a real community — families, assigned roles, general combat training plus individualized gift training. Tonally modeled on Velaris (Sarah J. Maas). Vane eventually realizes, much later in the series, what he's missed by dismissing them.
